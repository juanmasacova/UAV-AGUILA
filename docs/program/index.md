# Program

AGUILA runs as a stage-gated program. Each stage has written exit criteria, and nothing proceeds
until they are met. For a first-time designer the point is not ceremony — it is that skipping a
gate means finding out about a mistake in the air rather than on a bench.

- **[Stages](status.md)** — the board behind the home-page progress bar
- **[Decision log](decision-log.md)** — what has been decided, and on what basis
- **[Studies](studies/study-02-tiltwing.md)** — exploratory work, not adopted

## Program intent

Design, analyse, build and fly a VTOL-capable unmanned aircraft that carries a standard 33 cl
beverage can to a GPS waypoint, releases it, and returns — running the full engineering cycle
rather than assembling someone else's kit.

## MVP success criteria

In a single sortie, without intervention between arming and disarm, the aircraft:

1. Takes off vertically, or within a 1.5 m footprint
2. Transitions to wing-borne flight
3. Flies an uploaded waypoint mission of at least 500 m out
4. Releases a full 33 cl can within 10 m of a commanded drop point
5. Returns, transitions back, and lands within 3 m of the launch point

Anything beyond that — precision drop, autonomous landing on a marker, a second payload — is a
later block.

## A note on what is published here

The site fills in as decisions are actually taken. Where a page shows *not yet determined*, that
is the true state of the project, not an oversight.
