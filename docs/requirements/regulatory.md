# Regulatory

!!! warning "Not authoritative"
    A working checklist, not legal advice. Every item must be confirmed directly at
    [faa.gov](https://www.faa.gov/uas) and the answer recorded here before the first outdoor
    flight.

The aircraft's mass is not yet determined, but it will comfortably exceed the 250 g threshold, so
registration applies.

## Checklist

| Item | Requirement | Status |

## Notes

**Part 107 is worth holding regardless.** It removes ambiguity around the payload-drop mission,
and it is a real line on an aerospace CV.

**Visual line of sight** is required under Part 107. This is the substantive reason onboard video
was deleted from Block 1 rather than merely a weight saving — see
[D3](../program/decision-log.md#d3-fpv-video).
